📅 Booking & Payments

What is your booking process?

Securing your date is simple and streamlined:

  1. Availability Check: Contact us to see if your date is open.
  2. Custom Quote: We’ll send you a quote based on your event needs.
  3. Approval & Contract: Once you approve the quote, we will send over your contract.
  4. Deposit & Confirmation: Once the contract is signed and the deposit is paid, your date is officially locked in!
How much does it cost?

Our packages start at $249 for a 2-hour Digital-Only package. We also offer a popular Special Promo for $299, which includes 2hrs of service with both Digital & Prints.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash and Interac e-Transfer.

📸 Experience & Customization

Can I customize my photo template?

Absolutely! You are welcome to design your own template or overlay. We will be able to provide you a Canva link with samples.

Can photos be printed in color or black & white?

Yes, you can choose between vibrant full color or classic, timeless black and white.

How do guests receive their digital photos?

We host all photos and videos in a secure, private online gallery dedicated to your event. Guests can access, download, and share their media directly from the gallery, which remains active for three months after your event.

Can I add an extra hour?

Yes, of course, as long as there is no conflict with our next event for that day, if there is any.

🛠️ Setup & Technical Details

What kind of equipment do you use?

We believe in studio-quality results. Unlike setups that rely on iPads, our photo booths and mirror booths are powered by high-performance Intel i7 processors and paired with professional Canon R50 Mirrorless Cameras to deliver crisp, stunning images that outshine older DSLR setups.

How much space do you require?
  • Standard Photo/Mirror Booths: We require a 10' x 10' footprint but can accommodate spaces as small as 6' x 6'.
  • 360 Video Booth: We require a minimum footprint of 12' x 12' to ensure maximum safety and room for guests to enjoy.
How long does setup take?

It takes our team approximately 45 minutes to fully set up and test the equipment. We always arrive early to ensure everything is ready before your start time.

📍 Logistics & Add-Ons

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve clients anywhere in Ontario!

Do you accommodate outdoor events?

Yes, we love outdoor celebrations! To protect the high-end equipment, we just require a completely covered area, such as a tent or canopy, a flat/leveled surface or ground, and access to a reliable power source.

What other services or add-ons do you offer?

We love taking events to the next level. You can enhance your experience with:

  • 📞 Audio Guestbooks for heartfelt voice messages
  • 🔑 Custom Photo Keychains, the perfect personalized favor
  • ✨ Cold Sparklers & Ice Fog Effects for a spectacular grand entrance or first dance
